# WWE Creative Script: Rewriting WrestleMania XXX
## Match: The Undertaker vs. Brock Lesnar (with Paul Heyman)
**Stipulation:** The Streak vs. The Beast  
**Location:** Mercedes-Benz Superdome, New Orleans, LA  
**Result Goal:** The Streak Stays Alive (22-0) — The Ultimate Test of Immortality

---

### ACT I: THE COLLISION OF TITANS
* **Entrances**: 
  - Brock Lesnar enters first with Paul Heyman, walking like an alpha predator, bouncing on the apron in explosive fury. 
  - The lights plunge pitch black. A single bell tolls. 75,000 flashbulbs illuminate the Superdome mist as The Undertaker emerges, cloaked in his iconic duster, flanked by burning pyres shaped as Roman numerals "XXX".
* **The Opening Bell**:
  - Lesnar charges immediately like a silverback gorilla, driving Undertaker into the corner with thudding shoulder thrusts. 
  - Unlike real life—where an early concussion grounded the Phenom—The Undertaker fires back with vintage heavy right hands, rocking the former UFC Champion. 
  - Taker clotheslines Lesnar over the top rope to the floor, setting the pace fast, heavy, and furious. The crowd is immediately electric.

---

### ACT II: SHIFTING THE CARNAGE TO RINGSIDE
* **Commentary (Michael Cole, JBL, Jerry Lawler)**:
  - *Cole*: "We are seeing an Undertaker possessed tonight! He knows what Brock Lesnar is capable of!"
  - *JBL*: "The Beast has never been hit like this, Michael! But you don't poke a bear unless you're ready to bleed!"
* **The Turning Point**:
  - On the outside, Lesnar catches Taker mid-stride and drives his spine flush into the steel ring post. 
  - Lesnar delivers a devastating belly-to-belly suplex directly onto the padded concrete. The Superdome winces.
  - Back in the ring, Lesnar enters "Suplex City" prototype mode: German suplex, overhead belly-to-belly, punishing ground-and-pound elbows cutting open Taker's brow.
* **The Near Falls**:
  - Lesnar hoists Undertaker up: **F-5 #1**! 
  - The referee slides: *ONE... TWO... TH—KICKOUT!* 
  - Heyman screams on the outside, clutching his hair: "HOW?! HE'S NOT HUMAN, BROCK! PUT HIM DOWN!"

---

### ACT III: SUPERNATURAL RESILIENCE VS. UNCHECKED FURY
* **The Reversal**:
  - Lesnar goes for a second F-5, but Undertaker slips behind and executes a monstrous **Chokeslam**! 
  - Cover: *ONE... TWO... Lesnar powers out violently!*
  - Undertaker signals for the end. He scoops Lesnar: **Tombstone Piledriver**! 
  - *ONE... TWO... BROCK GETS A SHOULDER UP!*
  - The Superdome rises to its feet; nobody has ever kicked out of the Tombstone at WrestleMania except Shawn Michaels and Triple H. Brock joins elite company, and the tension hits boiling point.
* **The Climax & Heyman’s Blunder**:
  - Frustrated and desperate, Brock drags Undertaker outside and clears the Spanish announce table. Brock delivers **F-5 #2 THROUGH THE SPANISH TABLE**, exploding wood and monitors everywhere.
  - The referee begins the countout. Lesnar rolls into the ring, smirking, waiting for a countout or easy pin.
  - At the count of 9, Undertaker rises from the rubble like a zombie in the fog, rolling back into the ring! 
  - Brock cannot believe it. Heyman slides a steel chair into the ring behind the ref’s back, shouting: "End it, Brock! Murder the myth!"
  - Brock swings the steel chair—Undertaker duck-walks underneath, boots the chair directly into Lesnar’s face!
  - Lesnar staggers back, disoriented. Undertaker snares Lesnar by the throat, pulls him into the center, lifts all 286 pounds of Brock Lesnar high above his head, and delivers a thunderous, ring-shaking **JUMPING TOMBSTONE PILEDRIVER**!
  - Undertaker crosses Brock's arms and rolls his eyes into the back of his head:
  - **REFEREE**: *ONE! ... TWO! ... THREE!*

---

### ACT IV: THE AFTERMATH & FALLOUT
* **The Bell Rings**:
  - Howard Finkel / Justin Roberts: *"Here is your winner... AND STILL UNDEFEATED AT WRESTLEMANIA... THE UNDERTAKER!"*
  - The graphic flashes across the Mercedes-Benz Superdome screens: **22 - 0**.
  - 75,000 fans explode into a cathartic roar of celebration. Purple pyro floods the New Orleans sky.
* **The Angle / Next Storyline Hook**:
  - Undertaker drops to his knees in his iconic pose, lightning crackles on the titantron.
  - Brock Lesnar wakes up in absolute, uncontrollable rage. Rather than being diminished, Brock snaps into a bloodthirsty frenzy. He blindsides Paul Heyman with an F-5 out of pure disgust, then demolishes ringside equipment, security guards, and WWE staff.
  - The next night on *Raw*, Lesnar is completely unhinged—launching an unstoppable rampage through the entire locker room that makes him more terrifying and dangerous than ever, while preserving The Streak as an untarnished monument in wrestling history.
